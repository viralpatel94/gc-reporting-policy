Privacy Policy

This app is a private, internal reporting tool used only by Roshni Enterprises, Inc. (dba Great Clips #1719) and its owners. It reads the company's own QuickBooks Online accounting reports (read-only) to produce internal monthly summaries.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is stored only on the owners' private computer and accounts. Access can be revoked anytime from QuickBooks → Settings → Apps.
